ShakTalk Features

Real-time AI voice translation built for business and everyday life

ShakTalk is a live two-way AI voice translator that lets two people speak naturally in their own language and hear each other in theirs — instantly, with no interpreter, no typing, and no extra hardware. Here is what makes ShakTalk different.

1. Real-time two-way voice translation

Both people speak simultaneously in their own language. ShakTalk recognises each voice, translates the speech, and plays it in the other person's language in real time. There is no waiting, no turn-taking button, and no delay that breaks the flow of conversation.

This is not a one-way translation tool. It is a full two-way conversation — like a simultaneous interpreter in your pocket. AI translation. Check important details.

2. 17 supported languages

ShakTalk supports 17 languages with any-to-any pairing:

  • English, Hindi (हिन्दी), Arabic (العربية), Mandarin (中文), Cantonese (粵語)
  • Spanish (Español), French (Français), German (Deutsch), Italian (Italiano), Portuguese (Português)
  • Japanese (日本語), Korean (한국어), Russian (Русский), Turkish (Türkçe)
  • Urdu (اردو), Romanian (Română), Persian (فارسی)

Any language can be paired with any other — for example, Hindi to Mandarin for India–China trade, Arabic to Hindi for Gulf business, Spanish to English for travel, and so on. More languages are being added.

3. Connect by room code or QR scan

Starting a ShakTalk session is simple:

  • Room code — a 6-character code you share by WhatsApp, SMS, email, or just say out loud. The other person enters it and joins immediately.
  • QR scan — open the QR code in your app and let the other person scan it face to face. They are in the room in one tap.

The person joining does not need a ShakTalk account. They can join as a guest instantly.

4. Live subtitles on screen

As each person speaks, their translated words appear as live subtitles on both screens. This helps when audio is not clear — in a noisy factory floor, a busy hotel lobby, or a crowded trade fair — and gives both parties a written record of what was said during the call.

5. Conversation history and transcripts

ShakTalk saves a text transcript of your calls so you can review what was said later. This is useful for following up on orders, confirming details, or simply recalling a conversation from last week. Transcripts are stored privately in your account.

6. Works on Android — iOS coming soon

ShakTalk is available on Android via Google Play today. The iPhone (iOS) version is coming soon. Both apps are fully compatible — an Android user and an iPhone user can call each other without any issue.

7. No extra hardware

ShakTalk works on the phones both people already carry. There is no translation gadget to buy, no earpiece to set up, and no account for the person joining. Just install the app, share a code, and start talking.

Who uses ShakTalk

ShakTalk is used across industries where language is a barrier:

  • Importers and traders — supplier calls, factory visits, trade fairs (Hindi ↔ Mandarin, English ↔ Arabic)
  • Hotels and hospitality — international guests, tour desks, airport counters
  • Hospitals and clinics — foreign patients, medical tourism, emergency communication
  • Real estate — foreign buyers, NRI investors, overseas tenants
  • Business travellers — meetings, negotiations, daily needs abroad
