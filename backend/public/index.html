FAQ

Common questions

How does real-time voice translation work in ShakTalk?

ShakTalk uses AI speech recognition to convert what you say into text, then translates it instantly, and plays it as speech in the other person's language — all within a fraction of a second. Both people speak naturally in their own language and hear the other in theirs, with live subtitles on screen.

What languages does ShakTalk support?

ShakTalk currently supports 17 languages: English, Hindi, Arabic, Mandarin, Cantonese, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Urdu, Russian, Italian, Portuguese, Turkish, Romanian, and Persian. More languages are being added regularly.

Does ShakTalk work without internet?

ShakTalk requires an internet connection (Wi-Fi or mobile data) to perform AI translation in real time. It is not currently available offline. A stable connection ensures the best translation quality and lowest delay.

How do two people connect on ShakTalk?

There are two easy ways to connect: share a 6-character room code by WhatsApp, SMS, or email, or let the other person scan your QR code face to face. No account is required for the person joining — they can enter the room instantly.

Is ShakTalk suitable for business use?

Yes. ShakTalk is designed for real-world business communication — importers talking to overseas suppliers, hotels serving international guests, hospitals communicating with foreign patients, real estate agents dealing with foreign buyers, and call centres handling multilingual customers. AI translation. Check important details before finalising agreements.

Is ShakTalk available on iPhone and Android?

ShakTalk is available on Android via Google Play. The iOS version for iPhone is coming soon. The two apps are fully compatible — an Android user and an iPhone user can call each other without any issue.
