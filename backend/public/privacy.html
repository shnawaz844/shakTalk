This policy explains what information the ShakTalk app and website (together, "ShakTalk", "we", "us") collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. ShakTalk is provided by {{COMPANY_NAME}}, {{COMPANY_ADDRESS}}.

In short: we use your voice only to translate your call in real time. We keep your account details and your call transcripts so you can see your conversation history. We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising. You can delete your account and data at any time.

Legal bases

We process your information: to provide the service you signed up for (contract); based on your consent, which you give at sign-up, for real-time processing of your voice by our AI providers; to meet legal obligations; and for our legitimate interests in keeping ShakTalk secure and working. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account; this does not affect processing that already took place.

2. How translation works

During a call, your voice is sent over an encrypted connection to our servers and to our AI translation providers, which convert it into your partner's language and send back the translated speech and text. In Real-time mode, call audio is processed as a live stream and is not stored by ShakTalk. Our providers process it to deliver the service and do not use it to train their models under the paid business terms we use.

Translations are generated by AI and may contain mistakes. Please don't rely on ShakTalk alone for medical, legal, financial or emergency conversations.

3. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers who help us run ShakTalk, under contracts that limit how they can use it:

  • Google Cloud (Vertex AI / Gemini and Cloud Text-to-Speech): speech translation and the translated voice.
  • LiveKit: real-time audio connection between the phones in a call.
  • Clerk: sign-in and account management.
  • Supabase: database and file storage for transcripts, recordings and plans.
  • Amazon Web Services: hosting of our servers.
  • Apple and Google: app distribution and, when available, in-app payments.

The person you call sees and hears what you say in the call, its translation, and your chosen display language. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users or others. We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

4. Where your data is processed

Our providers operate data centres in several countries, including India, the United States and the European Union. When data is transferred across borders, we rely on our providers' contractual safeguards.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account details, profile settings, transcripts and recordings: until you delete them or your account.
  • Live call audio in Real-time mode: not stored after the translation is delivered.
  • Technical logs: up to 90 days.

When you delete your account, we delete your account and conversation data within 30 days. Copies in backups are removed within a further 90 days.

6. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live (for example, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or the EU/UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your data, withdraw consent, and complain to a data protection authority. You can:

  • Edit your profile in the app.
  • Delete your account and data: see Delete your account.
  • Contact us at {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} for any other request. We reply within 30 days.

7. Security

Calls and data are encrypted in transit. Access to stored data is restricted to the account owner and to authorised systems. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and the authorities of a breach where the law requires.

8. Age requirement

ShakTalk is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
