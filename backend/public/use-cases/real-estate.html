ShakTalk for Real Estate

Communicate with foreign buyers, NRI investors, and expat tenants in their language

International buyers are a major force in real estate markets across India, UAE, and beyond. NRI investors, Gulf-based buyers, and foreign expats often want to buy or rent property but face a communication gap when dealing with local agents who speak a different language. ShakTalk gives every real estate agent a live AI voice translator on their phone — no interpreter to book, no deal lost to a language barrier.

AI translation. Check important details. Always confirm property details, prices, legal terms, payment schedules, and contractual obligations in writing in a language both parties clearly understand. ShakTalk is a communication aid, not a legal instrument.

How real estate teams use ShakTalk

  • Property viewings — walk international buyers through a property while explaining features, layout, and neighbourhood in their language
  • Price negotiations — discuss offers, counter-offers, payment plans, and terms clearly across languages
  • NRI client calls — remote calls with investors based in the Gulf, UK, USA, or Canada who speak Hindi, Punjabi, Gujarati, or other languages
  • Expat tenant communication — Arabic, Russian, or Mandarin-speaking tenants dealing with English or Hindi-speaking property managers
  • Developer sales desks — Dubai and Indian developers presenting projects to foreign investor groups
  • Post-sale formalities — explaining registration, documentation, and handover processes to foreign buyers

Key language pairs for real estate

  • Arabic ↔ Hindi — Gulf-based buyers communicating with Indian agents
  • Arabic ↔ English — international real estate transactions in UAE and Middle East
  • Mandarin ↔ English — Chinese investors in Indian and global markets
  • Russian ↔ English — Russian buyers in UAE, India, and Europe
  • Hindi ↔ English — NRI buyers communicating with local Indian agents

What you get with ShakTalk

  • Live two-way voice translation — agent and buyer speak naturally, each hears the other in their language
  • Works on remote calls — not just face to face, but over the phone across countries
  • No app required for the buyer — they join via QR scan or room code
  • Conversation history — review and document what was discussed
  • Works on existing Android phones — available immediately
