ShakTalk for Hotels and Hospitality

Serve every international guest in their own language

Hospitality is built on communication. When a guest cannot explain what they need, or staff cannot understand a complaint, the experience suffers — and so does your reputation. ShakTalk gives every member of your hospitality team a live AI voice translator on their phone, so they can speak to international guests in their language, right now.

AI translation. Check important details. For medical situations, emergency services, or complex legal matters, engage a qualified professional. ShakTalk is a productivity tool for everyday hospitality communication.

How hospitality teams use ShakTalk

  • Hotel front desk — check-in, check-out, room requests, complaints, and directions for guests who don't speak English
  • Concierge and tour desk — explaining tours, booking activities, giving local recommendations to foreign guests
  • Restaurant and room service — taking food orders, explaining menu items, handling dietary requests across languages
  • Airport and transfer services — welcoming guests off the plane, explaining transfer arrangements, handling last-minute changes
  • Guest relations — handling complaints, special requests, or VIP guest communication in Arabic, Mandarin, Hindi, Russian, and more

Languages for hospitality

ShakTalk supports 17 languages covering the most common international guest profiles:

  • Arabic — Gulf tourists in India, Indian tourists in UAE
  • Mandarin and Cantonese — Chinese tourists across Asia and Europe
  • Russian — Russian-speaking travellers in India, UAE, Europe
  • Hindi — Indian guests at international hotels
  • Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese — European travellers worldwide
  • Japanese, Korean — East Asian tourists

What you get with ShakTalk

  • Live two-way voice translation — staff and guest speak naturally, each hears the other in their language
  • No app required for the guest — they join via QR scan or room code as a guest
  • Works on existing staff Android phones — no hardware to install
  • Live subtitles for noisy lobby environments
  • Conversation history for follow-up
