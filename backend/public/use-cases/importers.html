ShakTalk for Importers and Traders

Break the language barrier with your overseas suppliers — in real time

If you buy goods from China, Southeast Asia, or other markets, you know how much depends on being able to communicate clearly with your supplier. Pricing, quality, deadlines, quantities — everything that matters happens in conversation. When language is a barrier, misunderstandings happen and deals fall through.

ShakTalk gives importers and traders a live AI voice translator on their phone — speak in Hindi, your supplier hears Mandarin. They reply in Mandarin, you hear Hindi. No interpreter to book, no extra device, no typing.

AI translation. Check important details. Always confirm prices, quantities, delivery dates, and contract terms in writing in a language both parties understand before finalising any order.

How importers use ShakTalk

  • Daily supplier calls — routine order follow-ups, quality checks, and shipment tracking with Chinese factories
  • Trade fair negotiations — walk a booth at Canton Fair or similar, speak Hindi, let the supplier hear Mandarin
  • Factory visits — on-site meetings with production teams, supervisors, and quality control staff
  • Price negotiations — discuss MOQs, pricing tiers, and payment terms without a translator in the room
  • New supplier discovery — first calls with potential suppliers before the relationship is established

Languages for India–China trade

ShakTalk supports the most important language pairs for India–China sourcing:

  • Hindi ↔ Mandarin (中文) — the core India–China trade pair
  • English ↔ Mandarin — for English-speaking Indian exporters dealing with Chinese buyers
  • Hindi ↔ English — for internal team communication

What you get with ShakTalk

  • Live two-way voice translation — speak and hear simultaneously
  • Live subtitles on screen for noisy environments (factory floors, trade halls)
  • Conversation history — review what was said after the call
  • QR scan or room code connection — no sign-up required for the other party
  • Works on existing Android phones — no extra hardware
