ShakTalk for Hospitals and Clinics

Communicate with international patients in real time — without waiting for an interpreter

Medical tourism is growing rapidly, and hospitals serving international patients face a constant challenge: communicating clearly across language barriers when the stakes are high. Waiting for an interpreter to arrive — or relying on a family member with limited language skills — can delay treatment and cause misunderstandings.

ShakTalk gives doctors, nurses, and coordinators a live AI voice translator on their phone, available instantly, in 17 languages.

Important limitation. ShakTalk is an AI-powered productivity tool and is not a replacement for a qualified medical interpreter where one is legally required or where clinical precision is critical — for example, in informed consent, diagnosis, treatment instructions, or emergency triage. Always engage a certified medical interpreter for critical clinical communications. AI translation. Check important details.

How healthcare teams use ShakTalk

  • Initial consultation and registration — collecting patient history, explaining procedures, and gathering consent information from foreign patients
  • Medical tourism coordination — communicating with international patients before, during, and after treatment
  • Post-operative care instructions — explaining medication schedules, discharge instructions, and follow-up care in the patient's language
  • Family communication — speaking with the patient's accompanying family members who may not speak the local language
  • Pharmacy and discharge — explaining prescriptions, dosage, and next steps clearly
  • Routine ward communication — everyday communication with patients in long-term care

Languages for medical tourism

ShakTalk supports 17 languages. Key pairs for hospitals serving international patients:

  • Arabic ↔ English — Gulf patients in Indian and European hospitals
  • Hindi ↔ Arabic — Indian medical staff serving Arabic-speaking patients
  • Mandarin ↔ English — Chinese patients in India, UK, and Southeast Asia
  • Russian ↔ English — Russian-speaking patients abroad
  • Urdu ↔ English — Pakistani and diaspora patients

What you get with ShakTalk

  • Live two-way voice translation — doctor and patient speak naturally, each hears the other in their language
  • No app required for the patient — they join via QR scan or room code
  • Conversation history — review what was communicated during the session
  • Works on existing Android phones — no hardware procurement needed
  • Available 24/7 — no need to schedule an interpreter days in advance
