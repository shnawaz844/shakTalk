ShakTalk for Business Travellers

Navigate meetings, negotiations, and daily life abroad — in real time

Business travel puts you in conversations you didn't plan for — a last-minute meeting with a local partner, a supplier visit where nobody speaks English, a hotel check-in in a city where you don't know the language. ShakTalk gives you a live AI voice translator on your phone, available the moment you need it, in 17 languages.

AI translation. Check important details. Always confirm important agreements, prices, and instructions in writing in a language both parties clearly understand. ShakTalk is a communication tool, not a legal interpreter.

How business travellers use ShakTalk

  • Partner and client meetings — meetings where the other party prefers to speak in their language and you speak in yours
  • Factory and site visits — talking to production teams, supervisors, and site workers in their language
  • Negotiations — discussing prices, terms, and conditions across a language barrier without losing nuance to mistranslation
  • Networking at trade fairs — introducing yourself and your business to contacts at Canton Fair, Dubai Expo, or international exhibitions
  • Daily life abroad — hotels, restaurants, transport, shopping, and directions in countries where you don't speak the local language
  • Emergency situations — communicating needs quickly when you do not share a language with the person helping you

Languages for business travel

ShakTalk covers the most important business travel language pairs:

  • English ↔ Mandarin — business trips to China
  • English ↔ Arabic — travel to UAE, Saudi Arabia, Egypt, Qatar
  • Hindi ↔ Mandarin — Indian business travellers in China
  • English ↔ Japanese — visits to Japan
  • English ↔ Korean — meetings in South Korea
  • English ↔ Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian — European and Latin American trips
  • English ↔ Russian, Turkish — travel across Eastern Europe and Central Asia

Why ShakTalk for travel

  • No booking required — available instantly, 24/7, on your existing Android phone
  • No extra hardware — unlike translation earpieces or gadgets, ShakTalk uses phones both people already carry
  • No account required for the other person — they join via QR scan or room code as a guest
  • Works on calls and in person — use it on a voice call with a remote partner or face to face at a meeting table
  • Live subtitles — see translations on screen in noisy environments
