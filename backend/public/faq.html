Everything you need to know about ShakTalk real-time AI voice translation

How it works

How does real-time voice translation work?

ShakTalk uses AI speech recognition to convert what you say into text instantly, translates it to the other person's language, then speaks it out loud in their language — all within a fraction of a second. Both people speak naturally in their own language and hear the other in theirs, with live subtitles on screen. No typing, no switching apps, no interpreter needed.

How do two people connect on ShakTalk?

There are two easy ways:

  • Room code — share a 6-character code by WhatsApp, SMS, email, or even out loud. The other person enters it and joins immediately.
  • QR scan — open the QR code in your app and let the other person scan it face to face. They are in the room instantly.

The person joining does not need a ShakTalk account. They can enter as a guest using just the code or QR scan.

Can I use ShakTalk without internet?

No — ShakTalk requires a Wi-Fi or mobile data connection to perform AI translation in real time. A stable internet connection (at least 4G/LTE or a decent Wi-Fi signal) ensures the best translation quality and lowest delay.

Languages

What languages does ShakTalk support?

ShakTalk currently supports 17 languages: English, Hindi, Arabic, Mandarin, Cantonese, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Urdu, Russian, Italian, Portuguese, Turkish, Romanian, and Persian. Any language can be paired with any other — for example, Hindi to Mandarin, Arabic to Hindi, Spanish to English, and so on. More languages are added regularly.

Can I pair any two languages?

Yes. ShakTalk supports any combination of its 17 supported languages. One person selects their language, the other selects theirs, and the app translates both ways simultaneously.

Accuracy and reliability

How accurate is ShakTalk's translation?

ShakTalk delivers fast and practical AI-generated translations suitable for most everyday business and personal conversations. Translation quality depends on speaking clearly, ambient noise levels, and the complexity of what is being said. Is ShakTalk a replacement for a certified interpreter?

No. ShakTalk is a productivity tool designed for everyday business and personal communication. It is not a replacement for a certified human interpreter where one is legally required or where precision is critical — for example, in formal legal proceedings, medical diagnosis, or certified document translation.
