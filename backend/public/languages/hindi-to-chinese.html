How it works

ShakTalk uses AI speech recognition to understand what you say in Hindi, translate it instantly to Mandarin Chinese, and play it as speech for the other person — and does the same in reverse for their reply. The result is a natural, flowing conversation in two languages simultaneously.

Both people also see live subtitles on screen, which helps in noisy environments like factory floors or busy trade fair halls.

How to connect

Start a ShakTalk session in two ways:

  • Room code — share a 6-character code by WhatsApp or SMS. The Chinese supplier enters it and joins.
  • QR scan — open the QR code in your app and let the supplier scan it. They are connected instantly, no account needed.
