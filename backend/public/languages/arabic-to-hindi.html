How it works

ShakTalk uses AI speech recognition to understand Arabic speech, translate it to Hindi, and play it as speech for the other person — and does the same in reverse. The result is a natural, flowing conversation in two languages at once, with live subtitles visible on both screens.

How to connect

  • QR scan — open the QR code in your app and let the other person scan it face to face. No account required to join.

Frequently asked questions

Can ShakTalk translate Arabic to Hindi in real time?

Yes. ShakTalk provides live two-way AI voice translation between Arabic and Hindi. One person speaks in Arabic and the other hears Hindi, and vice versa — all in real time, without any typing or interpreter.

Does the Arabic-speaking person need an account?

No. The person joining your ShakTalk session does not need an account. They enter using a 6-character code or by scanning a QR code, and the session starts immediately.

Does ShakTalk support Gulf Arabic dialects?

ShakTalk's AI translation is trained on Modern Standard Arabic and common Gulf Arabic speech patterns. Translation quality may vary with heavy regional dialects. Speaking clearly and at a moderate pace helps the AI recognise speech accurately.

Can ShakTalk be used for Arabic to Urdu as well?

Yes. ShakTalk supports Urdu (اردو) as a separate language alongside Hindi, so Arabic ↔ Urdu translation is also available for communities where Urdu is preferred.
